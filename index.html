01

Leadership & management

Leading at national scale.

At Dar Al-Handasah I coordinated one of Egypt's largest development programs: more than 10,000 projects across four governorates (Minya, Assiut, Fayoum and Ismailia), with 200+ project engineers reporting into the planning office.

Every week the program's status went to sovereign-level meetings with the Egyptian Cabinet and the Presidency. That meant one version of the truth, assembled from thousands of field submissions, checked, and ready on time, every time. It is where I learned that big-data reporting is a leadership problem before it is a technical one.

Today at Arweqah I lead an 8-member PMO, including 2 AI engineers, overseeing about 20 projects in 4 portfolios for Saudi government entities, and I prepare the one-page executive reports the CEO and executive directors decide with.

03

Data architecture & engineering

One governed source of truth, built for governments.

I designed and built SQL Server and PostgreSQL databases for 10+ Saudi government entities, including the Ministries of Hajj & Umrah, Islamic Affairs, Culture, Human Resources and Energy, and the Social Development Bank.

Then I systematised how data enters them: integrations with Power Platform, Zoho, Odoo and Smartsheet, data-quality and validation rules that catch missing, late or inconsistent submissions, and a project-governance system on Power BI, Power Apps and Power Automate.

The result is a governed layer that every dashboard, report and AI assistant reads from, from project teams up to the CEO.
